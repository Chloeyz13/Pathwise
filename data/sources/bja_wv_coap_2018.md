# BJA FY2018 Comprehensive Opioid Abuse Program narrative, West Virginia
url: https://bja.ojp.gov/sites/g/files/xyckuh186/files/media/document/FY-2018-COAP-Program-Narrative-4.pdf

In 2015 West Virginia filled more opioid prescriptions than there are people: 1,049 opioid prescriptions per 1,000 West Virginians (IMS Institute for Health Informatics).

The state's economy has historically depended on coal; coal-impacted communities, primarily in the southern counties, carry the highest unemployment. Unemployment statewide (5.9%) is above the national average, with higher rates in the southern, coal-impacted counties.

West Virginia is rural, with limited literacy rates, and most counties have limited mental health treatment providers. Heavy rain and snow regularly limit road access, which further restricts access to services.

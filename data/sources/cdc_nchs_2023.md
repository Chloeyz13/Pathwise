# CDC NCHS Health E-Stat: Changes in Drug Overdose Mortality by State, 2022–2023
url: https://cdc.gov/nchs/data/hestat/drug-overdose/drug-overdose-mortality.pdf

The age-adjusted rate of drug overdose deaths in the United States decreased 4.0% from 2022 (32.6 per 100,000) to 2023 (31.3). The jurisdictions with the highest rates in 2023 were West Virginia (81.9) and the District of Columbia (60.7); the states with the lowest rates were Nebraska (9.0) and South Dakota (11.2).

Between 2022 and 2023 age-adjusted overdose death rates decreased in 20 states, did not change significantly in 25 states, and increased in 6 states: Alabama, Alaska, California, Nevada, Oregon and Washington.

Nationally the rate for deaths involving any opioid and synthetic opioids other than methadone (which includes fentanyl) decreased, while the rate for deaths involving psychostimulants such as methamphetamine and cocaine increased.

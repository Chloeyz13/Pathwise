# DEA Washington Division: West Virginia Drug Situation (unclassified)
url: https://www.dea.gov/sites/default/files/2018-07/DEA-WAS-DIR-024-17%20West%20Virginia%20Drug%20Situation%20-UNCLASSIFIED.pdf

Opioids remain the leading drug problem in West Virginia and are responsible for the majority of overdose deaths in the state. Fentanyl, fentanyl analogues and other novel opioids are now the main contributors to overdose deaths.

Much of the fentanyl and mixed heroin-fentanyl sold in West Virginia is sourced from Detroit. Users obtain controlled prescription drugs from licensed providers or from out-of-state traffickers who have expanded into West Virginia, and pharmacies both wittingly and unwittingly fill fraudulent prescriptions.

A contributing factor is unemployment: as of March 2016 West Virginia had the second-highest unemployment rate in the United States.

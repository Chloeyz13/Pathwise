# Fentanyl preference among rural people who inject drugs in West Virginia (PMC7487277)
url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487277

Overdose fatality rates in rural areas surpass those in urban areas, with West Virginia reporting the highest drug overdose death rate in the United States in 2017.

Among people who inject drugs who reported having ever used fentanyl (n=311), 43.4% reported preferring drugs containing fentanyl. Fentanyl preference is highly prevalent among rural people who inject drugs in West Virginia and is associated with factors that may exacerbate overdose risk.

Proponents of fentanyl cite its intense rush and its ability to overcome opioid tolerance. Because users have limited control over the amount of fentanyl in the drug supply, those who actively seek it may be at increased risk of overdose.
